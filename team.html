Project Overview

The academic purpose and direction of ChemNova X.

What is ChemNova X?

ChemNova X is a web-based Engineering Chemistry learning platform. It brings syllabus-oriented learning resources, numerical calculators, molecular exploration, practice activities and virtual laboratory simulations into one organised digital environment.

Why did we create it?

Chemistry includes theoretical principles, equations, numerical problems and practical concepts. The platform aims to make these topics easier to approach through structured explanations and interactive educational features.

Our Vision

To create an accessible and engaging digital environment where engineering students can learn fundamental chemistry, practise problem-solving and connect scientific concepts with engineering applications.

Our Mission

To organise academic learning materials and useful interactive tools in a simple platform that supports independent learning, revision and conceptual understanding.

Academic Foundation

Core topics aligned with the NIT U23CY101 Engineering Chemistry syllabus.

01

02
PolymersPolymerisation, classifications, properties and engineering applications
03
ElectrochemistryElectrochemical cells, electrode potentials, batteries and fuel cells
04
Corrosion and Its ControlCorrosion mechanisms, influencing factors and prevention methods
05
Fuels and CombustionCalorific values, combustion, coal analysis and fuel technology

The official syllabus and professor-provided textbook remain the primary academic references. Supplementary content is intended to extend understanding and should be distinguished from prescribed syllabus material.

Our Educational Impact

Making Chemistry More Accessible.

ChemNova X supports accessible digital education by organising learning materials and interactive resources in one place. Its goal is to help engineering students strengthen scientific understanding, numerical skills and practical awareness. This educational focus connects with the broader aims of quality education under United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 4.
