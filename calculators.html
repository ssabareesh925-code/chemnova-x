Total Water Hardness

Calculate total hardness expressed as calcium carbonate using the EDTA titration method.

FORMULA USED

Hardness = (V × M × 100,000) / S

V = EDTA volume; M = EDTA molarity; S = sample volume.

CHEMISTRY CONCEPT

The EDTA method is a complexometric titration used to estimate the combined calcium and magnesium hardness of water.
