EDTA Water Hardness Experiment

Estimate the total hardness of a water sample using complexometric titration.

● SIMULATION READY

EXPERIMENTAL APPARATUS

🧪EDTA Titration Setup

Virtual burette · Conical flask · Indicator

Solution: Wine red
In the actual experiment, Eriochrome Black T indicator changes from wine red to blue at the endpoint around pH 10.

EXPERIMENTAL VARIABLES

AWAITING EXPERIMENT

Adjust the variables and run the titration to estimate water hardness.

SCIENTIFIC PRINCIPLE

EDTA forms stable 1:1 complexes with calcium and magnesium ions. At the endpoint, the indicator changes colour when the metal ions have been complexed.

Hardness (mg/L as CaCO₃) = (V × M × 100,000) / S

V = EDTA volume in mL · M = EDTA molarity · S = sample volume in mL.

Addition polymerisation joins unsaturated monomers without eliminating small molecules. Condensation polymerisation typically involves reactions between functional groups, often releasing small molecules such as water.

n CH₂=CH₂ → [–CH₂–CH₂–]ₙ

Example: Ethene polymerises to form polyethylene.

GALVANIC CELL MODEL

🔋Zinc–Copper Electrochemical Cell

Zn | Zn²⁺ || Cu²⁺ | Cu

Electrons flow: Zn → Cu
The salt bridge completes the internal ionic circuit and helps maintain electrical neutrality.

CELL CONDITIONS

AWAITING CALCULATION

Adjust the electrode potentials to calculate the theoretical cell voltage.

SCIENTIFIC PRINCIPLE

A galvanic cell converts chemical energy into electrical energy through a spontaneous redox reaction.

E°cell = E°cathode − E°anode
Zn → Zn²⁺ + 2e⁻
Cu²⁺ + 2e⁻ → Cu

Under standard conditions, the Zn-Cu cell has a theoretical standard cell potential of approximately 1.10 V.

IRON CORROSION OBSERVATION

🔩Clean Iron Surface

No visible corrosion in this model.

Relative corrosion indicator: 0%

ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS

READY

Select conditions and observe the simulated corrosion tendency.

SCIENTIFIC PRINCIPLE

Iron corrosion is an electrochemical process involving anodic iron dissolution and cathodic reduction reactions. Oxygen and moisture commonly support atmospheric rusting.

Anode: Fe → Fe²⁺ + 2e⁻
Cathode: O₂ + 2H₂O + 4e⁻ → 4OH⁻

Protective coatings separate the metal from the environment. Zinc can also provide sacrificial protection.

COMBUSTION MODEL

🔥Fuel Combustion

This is a simplified educational model. It does not reproduce actual laboratory calorimeter measurements.

CALORIMETRIC VARIABLES

AWAITING CALCULATION

Set the experimental values and calculate the heat absorbed.

SCIENTIFIC PRINCIPLE

Combustion is an exothermic reaction. In a simplified calorimetry calculation, the heat gained by the water and calorimeter is related to the heat released by the fuel.

Q = (m × c × ΔT) / 1000
Calorific value = Q / fuel mass

m = water equivalent in grams · c = 4.184 J g⁻¹ °C⁻¹ · ΔT = temperature rise in °C.
